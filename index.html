What is Research.Data?

Research.data is a unified platform that brings together data engineers, data scientists, and business analysts in one shared environment.

Built on powerful open-source technologies, Rdata delivers fast, large-scale data processing at scale. Our Data Lake architecture ensures reliability, security, and performance while keeping costs low.

We've implemented comprehensive data governance to ensure all data is protected and secure — every access is tracked, audited, and controlled.
